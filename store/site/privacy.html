Full Window, Not Screen: Video Sizer: privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What is collected

Nothing. The extension does not collect, store elsewhere, sell or share any personal or browsing data.

Network requests

None. The extension makes no network requests, has no analytics and no account, and loads no remote code.

Settings

Your settings (the size you choose, the backdrop color, whether Esc exits, and the names of the sites you give their own settings) are saved in the browser’s own extension storage. If sync is on in your browser, the browser may sync them to your other devices through your browser account. The extension itself sends them nowhere.

Access to sites

The extension runs on every site so it can keep a page’s fullscreen request inside the tab. That work happens inside the page; nothing from the page is recorded or sent anywhere, and no browsing history is kept.
